Frequently Asked Questions

1. What exactly does Royal RoXn do?

We’re a done-for-you appointment setting company for Canadian Realtors. Our Canadian team identifies motivated sellers, qualifies them against your criteria, and books listing appointments directly on your calendar.

2. How is this different from buying leads?

Leads are just contact information. We deliver qualified, face-to-face listing appointments with homeowners who have expressed interest in selling and are ready to meet with a real estate agent. You simply attend the appointment, present your marketing strategy, and focus on winning the listing.

3. How quickly will I see results?

Most realtors receive their first qualified appointment within 7-10 business days after onboarding. Timelines may vary depending on your market and service area.

4. Is there a long-term contract?

Yes. Our minimum partnership is 3 months, giving us enough time to build consistent appointment flow and deliver measurable results. Onboarding is simple, and our cancellation policy is clear and transparent.

5. Which markets do you serve?

We work with Realtors across every Canadian province, from Vancouver Island to Halifax. Our appointment setters understand local market nuances.
